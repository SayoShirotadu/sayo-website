WINDOWS / FREE TRIAL AVAILABLE

Koi Garden

A little koi pond on your Windows desktop

Watch koi swim, feed them, and get to know them. Give your bonded fish names and call them over. Add seasonal decorations and a red bridge to make the pond your own—a quiet moment with koi between tasks.

v3.4.0 is here, with a free trial and no time limit.
Keep up to three koi in two varieties, feed them, and give them names. Your koi records carry over to the full version.

  • Choose where the koi appear or stay hidden, and place a red bridge wherever you like.
  • Bonded koi follow your cursor and play. Save a keepsake card for a koi you have named.
  • Discover koi in five sizes, including rare tiny and large fish.
  • Enjoy seasonal scenery with water lilies, waterside insects, and winter ice.
  • Record wildlife and leaves in the collection guide. Their names are revealed when you discover them.
  • The full version supports up to 50 koi. Adjust size, swimming speed, and effects, or use power-saving mode.

    ABOUT SAYO

    About

    Shirotadu Sayo / Karasuya Sayo

    I create as Shirotadu Sayo and stream as Karasuya Sayo. Born in Hokkaido, I now live in Gifu, Japan, and am a mother of two.

    I enjoy embroidery, sewing, wirework, resin accessories, collecting loose gemstones, illustration, and making software. I love animals, dragons, and creatures of all kinds; caring for animals is part of my everyday life.

    My work includes handmade earrings and hairpins, as well as apps such as Koi Garden, where koi swim across your screen.

    Talking does not always come easily, but I love a good chat. My streams wander from topic to topic, with some singing and games along the way.

    Whether you feel like talking or taking it easy, drop in whenever you like. There is room here to rest your wings.
